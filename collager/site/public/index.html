Make a collage

  1. Choose a shape and a layout for 2, 3, 4, or 6 photos. Pick a frame and color.
  2. Tap Add photos to pick pictures from your phone. Tap a photo in the preview or a thumbnail to replace just that picture. Drag a preview photo to reposition it. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in or out.
  3. For a caption, tap Add text, choose a color, then drag the text into place.
  4. Tap Share collage to send it or Download collage to save your PNG.

Your photos stay on your device.
